PlateForward
Rescue Food. Share Hope.

PlateForward is a food-waste redistribution platform that connects food donors such as restaurants, hostels, cafeterias and event organizers with receivers such as NGOs, volunteers and communities.

How it Works:
1.DONOR
2.Post Surplus Food
3.Food Listing
4.RECEIVER
5.Browse / Search
6.Claim Food
7.Pickup & Redistribution
8.Reduce Food Waste

Key Features:
1.User signup and login
2.Donor and receiver interfaces
3.Donate surplus food with quantity, category, location and pickup time
4.Browse and search available food
5.Claim available food
6.Manage donor listings
7.Protected application pages
8.Responsive desktop and mobile UI
9.Impact tracking for Meals Shared, Active Donors and Food Saved

Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
Prototype Storage: Browser LocalStorage
Planned Integration: Firebase Authentication, real SMS OTP and cloud database

Impact counters begin at 0 and are designed to increase from actual platform activity rather than using artificial numbers.

Vision

Turn surplus food into community impact — one rescued meal at a time.
